# Synopsis - Ginga Sengoku Gunyuuden Rai (SNES, 1996)

Strategy game after Joji Manabe's manga. Read by translators and story reviewers; spellings are in `glossary.md`.

## Setting
In the year Genma 3 the Holy Galactic Empire's Emperor Koki dies and the empire's 270-year rule ends. Warlords
carve up the galaxy. Danjo Hiki founds Gojo and sweeps the northern heavens (Hokuten); after his death his son
Gaira takes the throne as Emperor of Great Gojo, purges the able generals (Gen'i, Homei) and rules by fear.

## Playable factions (each has an intro, advisor talk on the map, battle quotes and an ending)
- **Nankinro** (Rai Ryuga): brash, kind-hearted hero from the south; strategist Shishin Daigakuya; wife Shimon
  and her attendant Ranran; generals Sho Shigen, Mokaku, Kobu, Unkai, Rin Sokuka. Goal: end the war, unite the
  galaxy under a just rule. Ending: Rai unites the galaxy.
- **Ren** (Rako): cold, ambitious lord; marries Yushun, the Nanban King's daughter, and becomes King of Nanten;
  later rebels (a turn other factions react to: "Rako has rebelled?!"). General Kyo Shisho (loyal, proud) and
  Teiho, chancellor Ryusho.
- **Nanten Alliance** (Masamune, the One-Eyed Dragon): heroine of Chi, sister of King Toramaru; right hand
  Hiryu. Leads the southern lords against Gojo.
- **Great Gojo** (Gaira): emperor, tyrant, brothers Gaizan and Gaien; woman general Roha; Five Tiger Generals
  (Osei, Hakki, Ryogenkaku, Buyo, Gi Shojo).
